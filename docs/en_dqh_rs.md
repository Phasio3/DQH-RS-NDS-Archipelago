# Dragon Quest Heroes: Rocket Slime

Dragon Quest Heroes: Rocket Slime is an action-adventure game developed by Tose and published by Square Enix for the Nintendo DS handheld video game console. The game lets you play Rocket, a slime from Boingburg that will have the great responsability to save its friends and family from the awfull Platypunks who captured them and destroy the town. You'll explore the world, save your friends and also battle against tanks using the objects you'll find in your adventures as ammunitions!

The Archipelago experience counts the bosses and each slimes as location checks. 

You can randomize the ammo for each tank battle.

The "items" you'll receive will be "slimes" which will increase the number of slimes in your town enabling the enlargement of the city.
The different zone in the game are unlockable and may cause problems in the progression of the game. This has to be proof-checked.
